01

Research prototype

AI-ASSISTED QUANTITATIVE RESEARCH

QUANTOS

DRY-RUN

An end-to-end research platform for turning point-in-time market data and alpha expressions into reproducible backtests, validated portfolios, gated dry-runs, and traceable research decisions.

What I built

  • FastAPI services and a React/TypeScript research console
  • Event-driven backtesting with no-lookahead checks
  • Risk gates, dry-run execution, and PnL reconciliation
  • Append-only, hash-chained audit records

Safety boundary

Live broker access and real-order submission are disabled by design. Results are research evidence—not claims of production trading performance.
